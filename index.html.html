The place

Taste, ambiance and hospitality in one room.

The Roy Fine Dine blends modern aesthetics with a warm, welcoming atmosphere. Elegant lighting, plush seating and thoughtful interiors make it right for family dinners, date nights and business meetings. The menu brings together aromatic biryani, sizzling tandoori, regional Indian curries and sophisticated continental plates.

Indian

Tandoor Da Roy, all-India curries, biryani and South Indian favourites.

Continental

Grills, steaks, seafood plates and fresh salads.

Global

Goan, Thai and Italian dishes, sandwiches and Kathi rolls.
